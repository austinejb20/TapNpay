About Us

At TapNPay, we're on a mission to make transport payments simple, secure, and stress-free for everyone.

We built TapNPay to help transportation businesses collect payments faster and run smoother operations. Using our smart TapNPay scanner, companies can say goodbye to manual fare collection and hello to real-time tracking, route management, and better control of their daily activities.

But it's not just about the businesses. Riders also benefit from a cashless, seamless experience — no more searching for change or waiting in line. Just tap and go.

Whether you're managing a fleet or catching a ride, TapNPay connects you to a better way to move.
